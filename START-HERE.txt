VOCALIA 1.0 — MAC / MACBOOK

Requires Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26 or later.
Drag Vocalia.app to Applications, then open it.
If macOS cannot verify the developer, follow the official per-app opening instructions in our guide:
https://github.com/Francoocicchetti/Vocalia/blob/v1.0.0/INSTALL-MAC.md

Choose your interface language and audio language, prepare the model and add recordings.
Full guide: https://github.com/Francoocicchetti/Vocalia/blob/v1.0.0/GUIDE.md
